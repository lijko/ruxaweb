axe-core rules -> RAWeb 1.1 criteria (SIP mapping first, RUXAL mapping for the other rules)
The 41 rules of the SIP simplified control method (accessibilite.public.lu, method v1.2.1) follow its table. The other rules are mapped on the official RAWeb 1.1 tests and glossary (e.g. ARIA textbox, combobox, slider, meter and progressbar are form fields: 11.1).
Format: rule-id: criteria. "1.1 / 1.2" = ONE of them, depending on the element (images: 1.1 if the image informs, 1.2 if decorative; links 6.1, buttons 11.9, form fields 11.1 or 11.2, other components 7.1). "3.1, 10.6" = BOTH. A rule not listed here has no RAWeb criterion in this mapping: choose from raweb-1.1-criteria.txt or ask the auditor.
